01 — Overview

      
A ledger that never loses a row.

      

        

          Inventory Management System is a console application built for a
          single job: keep an accurate, queryable record of stock — from the
          moment a product is added to the moment it's sold out. Every product
          lives twice in memory: once in an std::map<int, Product>
          for instant lookups by ID, and once as a sortable std::vector
          whenever you need it ordered by name, price, or quantity.
        

        

          

            O(log n)
            map insert · search · delete
          

          

            O(n log n)
            std::sort by name, price, quantity
          

          

            O(log n)
            manual binary search by ID
          

        

      

    

  


  
  

    

      
02 — Features

      
Fifteen operations. One clean menu.


      


        

          SKU-01
          
Full CRUD

          
Add, display, update, and delete products, with duplicate-ID and negative-value validation baked in.

        


        

          SKU-02
          
Sell & restock

          
Move stock in and out. Selling more than you have on hand is rejected — the ledger never goes negative.

        


        

          SKU-03
          
Search, three ways

          
O(log n) map lookup by ID, plus linear search by name or category for looser queries.

        


        

          SKU-04
          
Sort on demand

          
std::sort the current inventory by name, price, or quantity without touching the underlying store.

        


        

          SKU-05
          
Binary search

          
A hand-written binary search over a sorted vector — a working reference implementation, not just map::find.

        


        

          SKU-06
          
Low-stock alerts

          
Flags every product under the restock threshold, so nothing runs out unnoticed.

        


        

          SKU-07
          
Inventory reports

          
Total units, total value, priciest and cheapest items, highest and lowest stock — exportable to report.txt.

        


        

          SKU-08
          
CSV persistence

          
Loads inventory.csv on launch, writes it on exit, and creates the file automatically if it's missing.
